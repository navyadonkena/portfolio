What "no slop" means in practice

Not a slogan. Specific rules baked into every workflow I build or run.

  • Real inputs. Content starts from your product knowledge, customer language, and search data, not a blank prompt.
  • A human at the right points. AI does the heavy lifting; a person decides the angle and signs off on facts.
  • Voice that holds. Style rules and examples keep piece number 50 sounding like piece number 1.
  • Built to be cited. Structure, entities, and schema so answer engines can quote the page.
  • Measured. Rankings, citations, and pipeline are tracked, so the system improves instead of just producing.
