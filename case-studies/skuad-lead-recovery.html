What was broken

  • Lead volume was dropping. Pages that once brought leads were not converting at the same level anymore.
  • Important pages needed sharper intent match. EOR, payroll, compliance, and country pages needed clearer answers for high-intent queries.
  • AI discoverability was missing. Skuad also needed content structured for answer engines, not only traditional search rankings.
