What was broken

What I owned

Lead volume was dropping. Pages that once brought leads were not converting at the same level anymore.
Recovery strategy. Identified gaps and opportunities in high-intent pages for AEO and search ranking.
Weak intent match. EOR, payroll, compliance, and country pages needed clearer answers for high-intent queries.
Content system. Built a quality-driven content process, writer workflow, and research automations for compliance-heavy information.
AI discoverability was missing. Content wasn't structured for answer engines, only traditional search rankings.
Editorial management. Briefed, edited, managed, and guided five writers end to end.
