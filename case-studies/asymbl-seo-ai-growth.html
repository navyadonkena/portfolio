How I helped Asymbl turn a rebrand into stronger search and AI visibility.

Asymbl was sharpening its positioning in recruiting and workforce orchestration, and needed content that could turn those new category gaps into authority. The goal was to improve website traffic, search discoverability, and AI discoverability through a mix of keyword research, long-form SEO blogs, and thought-leadership content.

What Asymbl needed

  • Turn new positioning into authority. The rebrand created clearer category gaps around recruiting orchestration, Salesforce recruiting, AI hiring, and modern ATS workflows.
  • Capture high-intent search demand. The content program needed keyword research and SEO-led blogs that could rank for recruiting and HR-tech searches.
  • Earn AI discoverability. The content also had to explain topics clearly enough to support visibility in AI-led discovery, not only traditional Google results.

Growth signals from the content program

The strongest movement came from impressions, clicks, and lead-generating blog content.

  • Keyword research: Mapped search demand across recruiting automation, ATS, CRM, Salesforce recruiting, AI hiring, and workforce orchestration topics.
