What Asymbl needed

  • Turn new positioning into authority. The rebrand created clearer category gaps around recruiting orchestration, Salesforce recruiting, AI hiring, and modern ATS workflows.
  • Capture high-intent search demand. The content program needed keyword research and SEO-led blogs that could rank for recruiting and HR-tech searches.
  • Earn AI discoverability. The content also had to explain topics clearly enough to support visibility in AI-led discovery, not only traditional Google results.

The growth showed up across search, AI, and lead signals

Impressions and clicks grew from May to August, while selected blogs started generating lead events and AI-citation visibility.

Search visibility more than tripled across the new content set.

Impressions grew from 26,665 in May to 96,015 in August, while clicks grew from 23 to 82 in the same period. September is already tracking 3,216 impressions and 6 clicks through the 2nd.

The impact reached far beyond the blog

Site-wide Google Search Console data — every page, not only blogs — shows the same growth curve at a much bigger scale, and blogs are what drove it.

Site-wide impressions grew 124x after the blog program began.

On February 1, 2026 — before the blog program started — the site logged just 978 impressions and 19 clicks. By August, monthly totals reached 121,727 impressions and 720 clicks.
