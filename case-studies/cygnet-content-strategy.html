What Cygnet needed

What I owned

Faster keyword growth Cygnet was ranking for just 33 keywords in Google's top 10, with limited coverage across its core data, AI, and cloud categories.
SEO strategy & keyword research Built a keyword strategy across branded and non-branded terms, prioritizing high-intent topics in data analytics, AI strategy, and cloud migration.
Credibility with technical buyers An audience of data, AI, and cloud decision-makers needed content with real depth, not surface-level marketing copy.
Long-form technical content Researched and wrote 9 in-depth guides that translated complex technical topics into buyer-ready content, feeding both search rankings and AI citations.
Visibility in AI answer engines With ChatGPT and Perplexity emerging as a discovery channel, Cygnet had no strategy or measurement in place for AI-referred traffic.
AI search measurement Tracked AI-referred sessions and conversions separately from organic, surfacing a 300% lift in form submissions from this channel.
